Chi-squared test (6m attendance): chi2=4.258, nominal p=0.0391

IMPORTANT — significance caveat:
  Six-month visit attendance was not the prespecified primary BETTER-BP endpoint
  (which was objective medication adherence by bottle-opening). Multiple outcomes
  are examined in this script. The nominal p-value (0.039) is therefore exploratory
  and does not survive a Bonferroni correction for all comparisons conducted.

Recommended interpretation of the 6m vs 12m attendance pattern:
  Randomization to the incentive intervention was associated with higher six-month
  visit attendance, corresponding to the active incentive period, whereas no
  attendance difference was observed at 12 months. Because visit attendance was
  analyzed as a secondary retrospective endpoint and the intervention targeted
  medication adherence rather than visit participation, this finding is interpreted
  as evidence of a potentially time-limited engagement effect requiring external
  confirmation. The 12-month null result is substantively important: universally
  providing incentives did not produce a sustained attendance advantage, motivating
  the adaptive targeting approach in TRUST-CT.

======================================================================
  §7  Outcomes NOT Reproducible from Public Data
======================================================================

The following published results CANNOT be reproduced from the public REDCap export:

  1. PRIMARY ADHERENCE OUTCOME: Published report shows 71.0% vs 34.1% adequate
     adherence (bottle opening ≥80% of days). The AdhereTech bottle-opening
     event stream is NOT included in the public release; only bottle setup/return
     records are available. This outcome is therefore computationally unverifiable.

  2. LOTTERY OUTCOMES: The $5 and $50 lottery randomizations and payout records
     are not in the public dataset. Per-participant incentive amounts cannot be
     reconstructed.

  3. SEQUENTIAL ACTION LOGS: No daily or weekly per-participant policy action
     propensity records are available. DQN or bandit policies cannot be trained
     on observed trial actions.

These limitations are documented in the TRUST-CT CMPB revision. All claims
about BETTER-BP in the paper are restricted to visit-level outcomes above.
